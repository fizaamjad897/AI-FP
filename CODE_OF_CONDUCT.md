# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Use constructive, evidence-based feedback, acknowledge others' work, and welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, and deliberate misrepresentation of research are not acceptable. Do not share patient-identifying information in this project.

The maintainer, Fiza Amjad ([@fizaamjad897](https://github.com/fizaamjad897)), may edit or remove abusive content, warn participants, or restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled with discretion. Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools for harassment and the private security-reporting route in SECURITY.md for vulnerabilities or exposed data. Do not create a public issue containing private details.
