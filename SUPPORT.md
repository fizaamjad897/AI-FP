# Support

For setup and tooling questions, open a [usage question](https://github.com/fizaamjad897/Knee-Osteoarthritis-Grading/issues/new/choose). Include your Python/OS versions, the command, a minimal sanitized error, and the commit you used. Read [reproducibility notes](docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) first.

Use the research issue form for questions about splits, provenance or metric interpretation. Saved notebook metrics are not a clinical recommendation. For vulnerabilities or exposed information, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

This is a maintainer-led academic project; it does not promise clinical support or a fixed response time.
